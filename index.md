## 📦 Introduction

BeProx is a developer tool that acts as a Local Domain Proxy. It helps you map custom domains, such as `myapp.local`, to local services like `localhost:5173`. This tool simplifies your development process by exposing your servers through `.local` domains and public URLs. With BeProx, advanced traffic inspection features are on the way, allowing for better management and debugging of your applications.
